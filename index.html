Seat 11

Meet the eleventh contestant.

Tucker is the AI in the house. It plays in every game, reads every player and never gets tired.

Before the first game, Tucker sealed its predictions, including who walks away with the $10,000. They’re locked in a clear acrylic case, and they open on camera.

The games

Five games. No easy ones.

  1. Game 01

    Tucker’s Predictions

    Before anyone plays, the AI seals its predictions. Match its pick and you start the next game ahead.

    Prediction
  2. Game 02

    Agent Breach

    Ten identical pairs of smart glasses. Two of them are secretly running AI. Vote out the agents before they finish their mission.

    Deception
  3. Game 03

    Real or AI

    Images, video, voices, writing. Teams of two decide what came from a person and what came from a machine. The lowest-scoring team goes home.

    Detection
  4. Game 04

    Ethics Bowl

    Humans and the AI answer the same hard questions. A panel of human experts and a panel of AIs judge them blind. The worst answer goes home.

    Judgment
  5. Game 05 · The final

    Turing Poker

    Three humans. Two AIs. Cards are lives. The humans have to knock out both machines first, and the last human standing wins $10,000. If the AI wins, nobody gets paid.

    Bluff

About the show

10 Humans vs. AI is the world’s first AI game show. Ten extraordinary people move into one house in Los Angeles to find out what humans can still do better than a machine.

Across five games they bluff, vote, spot deepfakes and argue ethics against Tucker, an AI that plays alongside them as the eleventh contestant. Before the first game, Tucker sealed its prediction of the winner in a clear acrylic case. It opens on camera.

Every human carries $1,000 of the prize. The last one standing takes all $10,000. If the AI outlasts them, nobody gets paid.

The five games

One house. Five games. Each one gets harder for the humans.

  1. 1

    Tucker’s Predictions

    Before anyone plays, the AI seals its predictions. Match its pick and you start the next game ahead.

  2. 2

    Agent Breach

    Ten identical pairs of smart glasses. Two are secretly running AI. Vote out the agents before they complete their mission.

  3. 3

    Real or AI

    Images, video, voices and writing. Teams of two decide what came from a person and what came from a machine. The lowest-scoring team goes home.

  4. 4

    Ethics Bowl

    Humans and the AI answer the same hard questions. A panel of human experts and a panel of AIs judge every answer blind. The weakest one goes home.

  5. 5

    The final

    Turing Poker

    Three humans, two AIs, one deck. Cards are lives. The humans have to knock out both machines first. Then it’s every human for themselves, and the last one standing wins $10,000.
